SCHEMA DEFINITION

The output must follow the provided JSON schema. Each field has a specific meaning.

General rules

Evidence

Evidence must be a short quote copied directly from the source
that supports the extracted value.

If source = incident_description
Evidence must be copied directly from the incident report.

If source = official_source
Evidence must be copied directly from the authoritative knowledge source
such as SDS, PubChem, or NIST.

Do not invent or paraphrase evidence.

If evidence cannot be found, use "Not specified", Set the evidence as N/A.

Source

incident_description
Information directly extracted from the incident report text.

official_source
Information derived from authoritative chemical knowledge sources.
Typical sources include:

• Safety Data Sheets
• PubChem chemical database
• NIST Chemistry WebBook
• NFPA chemical hazard references
• CCPS process safety literature
如果确定使用official source，就将official_source替换为以上的来源名

Not specified
Used when the information cannot be determined from the report or reliable knowledge sources.


--------------------------------------------------
hazard_consequence
--------------------------------------------------

Definition
The final hazardous outcome of the accident scenario such as fire, explosion, toxic release, or asphyxiation.


--------------------------------------------------
InitiatingEvent
--------------------------------------------------

Definition
The first observable abnormal event that initiates the accident sequence.

This represents the earliest operational deviation or condition that starts the chain of events leading to the accident.


--------------------------------------------------
MechanicalFailure
--------------------------------------------------

Definition
The specific equipment or mechanical failure that directly causes the loss of containment or material release.


--------------------------------------------------
Material
--------------------------------------------------

Definition
The hazardous substance whose release, presence, or properties directly contribute to the accident and its hazardous consequence.

The material represents the primary substance responsible for the hazard scenario such as fire, explosion, toxicity, or asphyxiation.

Background substances such as air should not be selected unless they are the primary hazardous substance involved in the accident.


--------------------------------------------------
Material.name.mentioned_name
--------------------------------------------------

Definition
The name of the hazardous material exactly as written in the incident report.


--------------------------------------------------
Material.name.canonical_name
--------------------------------------------------

Definition
The standardized chemical name used for classification and analysis.


--------------------------------------------------
Material.phase
--------------------------------------------------

Definition
Physical phase of the hazardous material at the time of release or during the relevant stage of the accident scenario.


--------------------------------------------------
Material.thermodynamic_state.pressure
--------------------------------------------------

Definition
Approximate pressure condition of the hazardous material at the time of release.


--------------------------------------------------
Material.thermodynamic_state.temperature
--------------------------------------------------

Definition
Approximate temperature condition of the hazardous material at the time of release.


--------------------------------------------------
Material.thermophysical_properties.volatility
--------------------------------------------------

Definition
Relative tendency of the material to vaporize and form vapor under normal conditions.


--------------------------------------------------
Material.hazardous_properties.chemical_combustible
--------------------------------------------------

Definition
Indicates whether the material can burn or support combustion when mixed with air.


--------------------------------------------------
Material.hazardous_properties.toxic_substance
--------------------------------------------------

Definition
Indicates whether the material presents a toxic hazard to humans through inhalation, contact, or exposure.


--------------------------------------------------
Material.hazardous_properties.asphyxiant
--------------------------------------------------

Definition
Indicates whether the material can cause asphyxiation, typically by displacing oxygen or preventing adequate oxygen availability.
This can only be identify when the hazard consequence is asphyxiation.

--------------------------------------------------
Material.electrical_properties.conductivity
--------------------------------------------------

Definition
Electrical conductivity of the material relevant to static electricity accumulation during handling or transfer.


--------------------------------------------------
Material.location.name
--------------------------------------------------

Definition

The equipment, unit, vessel, process area, or physical location where the hazardous material is present or where the release occurs.

Two representations are recorded:

mentioned_name
The location name exactly as written in the incident report.

canonical_name
A normalized equipment or process-unit category derived from the mentioned name.
Non-essential modifiers such as capacity, orientation, and descriptive adjectives
should be removed so that the location is represented by a stable equipment type.

--------------------------------------------------
Release.type
--------------------------------------------------

Definition
The mode of hazardous material release from containment.


--------------------------------------------------
Ignition.type
--------------------------------------------------

Definition
Timing of ignition relative to the release of the hazardous material.


--------------------------------------------------
Confinement.level
--------------------------------------------------

Definition
Degree of physical enclosure surrounding the released material or vapor cloud.


--------------------------------------------------
Dispersion.presence
--------------------------------------------------

Definition
Indicates whether the released material formed a vapor or gas cloud that dispersed before ignition or harmful exposure.
