INPUT:

Let's think step by step:

1. Normalize and align conditions
   - Extract concrete facts from SCENARIO related to CONDITIONS.
   - For each fact, express its mapping explicitly as a causal relation:
     "<scenario description> causes <condition>"
   - These lines become the first causal steps of each hazard chain.
   - Example:
     "Dust collector malfunction causes Mechanical failure"
     "Indoor pre-mix room causes High confinement"
     "Gilsonite powder causes Solid"
     "Gilsonite powder causes Chemical combustible"

   -Extract concrete facts from SCENARIO related to CONDITIONS, including the initial events.
   -Map SCENARIO facts to CONDITIONS. Only keep conditions that (a) are supported by SCENARIO and (b) can obtain a physically sensible incoming cause from SCENARIO context or an upstream condition allowed by RULES.
   -If a candidate condition has no plausible physical cause, do not include it. Initiating events (e.g., “Mechanical Failure”, “High pressure”) may start chains if justified by SCENARIO.

2. Identify all plausible hazards (multiple allowed)
   -If SCENARIO explicitly states hazards that appear in HAZARDS, include them.
   -Additionally, include any other hazard in HAZARDS that is reachable from the aligned upstream conditions through valid RULES edges and remains physically consistent with SCENARIO (phase, confinement, ignition timing, pressure/temperature).

3. Build causal chain per hazard using RULES
   - Start each chain from the most upstream initiating nodes supported by SCENARIO.
   - Include intermediate hazard nodes (e.g., Flash fire) if they physically occur before the target hazard in the SCENARIO.
   - Do not truncate the chain at intermediate hazards; continue chaining until the final hazard node defined by RULES.

4. Consistency checks
   -Every included node must come from RULES/CONDITIONS/HAZARDS.
   -Each step must be physically reasonable for the material phase and operating/environmental conditions in SCENARIO.
   -Do not include commentary—only the causal lines.

OUTPUT (strict):

Output one block per hazard.

Begin each block with a header line:
HAZARD: <hazard_name>

Then list the causal chain lines in the exact format below. End the last line at the hazard node.
