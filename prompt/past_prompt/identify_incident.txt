You are a professional process safety analyst.

INPUT:
REPORT_TEXT:
{report_text}

TASK:
Extract the **exact original text** of the section that describes what happened 
in the incident (i.e., the sequence of physical events), typically titled:

- "INCIDENT DESCRIPTION"
- "DESCRIPTION OF INCIDENT"
- "INCIDENT SUMMARY"
- "INCIDENT OVERVIEW"
- "BACKGROUND – WHAT HAPPENED"
- or any reasonably equivalent heading.

REQUIREMENTS:
- Return **the complete section**, starting from the section header 
  until the next top-level section header of equal hierarchy.
- If the section contains subsection headers (e.g., “2.1”, “2.a”), keep them.
- **Remove only figure captions, table captions, photo captions, page headers/footers.**
- Do **NOT** remove valid narrative text, even if mixed across pages.
- Do **NOT** summarize, paraphrase, reorganize, or interpret.
- Output **only the cleaned raw text**, nothing else.
- If multiple candidate sections exist, choose the one that most clearly 
  describes the chronological physical evolution of the incident.

OUTPUT:
Only the raw extracted incident description text.
