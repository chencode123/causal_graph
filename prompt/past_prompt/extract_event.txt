----------------------------------------

2. EQUIPMENT NORMALIZATION

- Standardize equipment terminology to the most common engineering term.
- Remove non-functional descriptive adjectives.

Examples:
  "earthen spill containment area"
  "spill containment"
  "secondary containment area"
  "diked containment zone"
  → "diked containment area"

  "storage tank vapor space"
  "vapor space above liquid"
  → "tank headspace"

  "above-ground storage tank"
  → "storage tank"

- Do NOT change equipment class.

----------------------------------------

3. LOCATION NORMALIZATION

- Normalize location wording to the most functionally accurate term.
- Remove directional or size modifiers unless functionally relevant.

Examples:
  "inside the enclosed processing building"
  → "processing building"

  "upper section of the tank"
  → "tank interior"

  "area above the diked containment"
  → "diked containment area"

----------------------------------------

4. CONSISTENCY REQUIREMENT

If multiple synonymous forms refer to the same material, equipment, or location,
they MUST be written identically in the output.

----------------------------------------

5. DO NOT normalize or alter:

- Mechanism explanations
- Release behavior descriptions
