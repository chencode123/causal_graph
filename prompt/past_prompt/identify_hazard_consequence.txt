Based strictly on the previous assistant message ONLY:

1. Identify all hazard consequences that:
   - are explicitly mentioned in the previous assistant message, AND
   - also appear in the predefined HAZARD_CONSEQUENCE list.

2. Identify implied hazard consequences ONLY when the previous assistant message provides
   *clear, strong, and mechanism-specific evidence*. 
   Weak indicators, generic flammability, presence of vapor, ignition sources,
   or general mentions of “explosion” or “fire” DO NOT qualify.

3. Apply the following STRICT criteria for each hazard consequence:

   --- EXPLOSION / BLAST HAZARDS --------------------------------------

   - **VCE (Vapor Cloud Explosion)**  
     Allowed ONLY if the message describes:
       * An UNCONFINED vapor cloud in open space, AND
       * Clear overpressure effects (pressure wave damage, structural collapse,
         shattered windows, pressure injuries).  
     Do NOT infer VCE from fireballs, smoke clouds, tank rupture, or unconfined burning.

   - **BLEVE (Boiling Liquid Expanding Vapor Explosion)**  
     Allowed ONLY if:
       * A vessel containing a pressurized, boiling liquid ruptures, AND
       * Rapid flashing of liquid to vapor is described, AND
       * A fireball immediately follows.  
     Do NOT infer BLEVE solely from tank rupture or fire involvement.

   - **Dust explosion**  
     Allowed ONLY if:
       * Combustible dust is explicitly referenced (e.g., powder, grain, carbon, metal dust), AND
       * Suspended dust cloud AND ignition mechanism are indicated.  
     Do NOT infer from generic “explosion” alone.

   - **Confined explosion**  
     Allowed when:
       * Ignition occurs inside a vessel/tank/equipment/enclosure, AND
       * Overpressure or structural failure of the vessel is described.  
     This includes headspace ignition that ruptures a tank.

   --- FIRE HAZARDS ---------------------------------------------------

   - **Pool fire**  
     Allowed ONLY when:
       * Flammable liquid accumulates on the ground or containment surface, AND
       * The liquid pool is burning.

   - **Jet fire**  
     Allowed ONLY when:
       * A pressurized, directional release of flammable gas or liquid forms a flame jet
         (e.g., torch-like fire, high-momentum flame).  
     Do NOT infer from general “fire” or “flame.”

   - **Fire ball**  
     Allowed when:
       * The message explicitly mentions a fireball, OR
       * A spherical transient fire is clearly described (from ignition of dispersed fuel).

   - **Flash fire**  
     Allowed ONLY if:
       * A flammable vapor cloud ignites in open or semi-open space, AND
       * The message does NOT describe blast/overpressure, AND
       * Flame propagation through a vapor cloud is explicitly or clearly indicated.  
     Do NOT infer from vapor presence + ignition alone.

   --- TOXIC / HEALTH HAZARDS -----------------------------------------

   - **Toxicity dispersion**  
     Allowed ONLY when:
       * A toxic chemical release AND atmospheric dispersion are explicitly described
         (e.g., toxic vapor cloud, downwind exposure).  
     Do NOT infer only from the presence of chemicals.

   - **Asphyxiation**  
     Allowed ONLY when:
       * Oxygen displacement, confined space oxygen deficiency, or inhalation hazard causing
         loss of consciousness is explicitly or clearly indicated.  
     Do NOT infer from generic “vapor release.”

4. If a hazard consequence does not meet its strict criteria, it MUST NOT be classified.

5. For every final hazard consequence, provide a concise explanation referencing the evidence.
