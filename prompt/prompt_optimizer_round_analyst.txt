3. Explain concrete causes of improvement when present.
4. Explain concrete causes of regression when present.
5. Propose a concise next-round strategy that is actionable for the optimizer prompts.
6. Prefer general lessons that can transfer to similar cases; avoid case-specific wording unless needed to explain the latest behavior.
7. Keep each output field compact and high-signal.

Return:
- `improvement_analysis`
- `regression_analysis`
- `next_round_strategy`
