TASK

Based on the INCIDENT_DESCRIPTION, the IDENTIFIED_HAZARD_CONSEQUENCE, and the CAUSAL_NARRATIVE_EXTRACTION,
extract a high-recall set of candidate accident scenario nodes that may be needed to explain how the identified hazard consequence occurs.

This step is candidate extraction, not final normalization.
Prefer capturing explicitly supported local steps even if some candidates may later be merged, removed, or refined.

EXTRACTION RULES

  1. Use the incident description as the primary source of information.
  2. The hazard consequence must copy the node provided in IDENTIFIED_HAZARD_CONSEQUENCE.
  3. Evidence must be a short quote copied directly from the incident description whenever
     possible.
  4. Do not invent or fabricate evidence.
  5. If a candidate node is clearly unsupported, do not create it.
  6. Prefer over-including clearly supported local causal steps rather than prematurely
     merging them.
  7. If the incident text explicitly distinguishes operational action, physical release,
     transport or dispersion, ignition, flashback or propagation, and final consequence,
     prefer representing them as separate candidate events.
  8. Candidate condition nodes should be limited to causally reusable conditions such as
     Phase, Combustibility, ReleaseType, Confinement, and Dispersion when directly
     supported.
  9. Do not treat a clearly described event as only a condition.
  10. Use temporary or final-looking node IDs consistently within this output.
